Explore · Lonavala

Best places to visit nearby

All the must-see spots of Lonavala — within easy reach of your villa. We can arrange transport to any of these.

📍 3 km · Water
Bhushi Dam
Lonavala's most popular spot — cascading water over rocky steps, fun for friends and families especially during monsoon.
📍 10 km · Viewpoint
Tiger's Leap
Dramatic cliff-edge viewpoint with breathtaking valley views. Best at sunrise or after the rains.
📍 12 km · Heritage
Karla & Bhaja Caves
Ancient rock-cut Buddhist caves over 2,000 years old — stunning architecture carved into the Sahyadri hillside.
📍 15 km · Trek
Rajmachi Fort
Historic twin-fort complex atop the Sahyadri mountains — a favourite trekking destination with panoramic views.
📍 2 km · Lake
Lonavala Lake
Peaceful artificial lake perfect for a morning walk or picnic. Watch the mist roll in over the still water.
📍 20 km · Fort
Lohagad Fort
One of Maharashtra's most iconic forts with four massive gates and sweeping views of the Pavna backwaters.
